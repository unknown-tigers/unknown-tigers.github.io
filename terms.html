Terms & privacy

UNKNOWN is an independent curatorial art platform. This website is presently a public preview. Artist registration, artwork uploads, and application submission are not yet available through this website.

Any future submission of artwork will require the submitter to confirm ownership or permission to share it. Submission will not guarantee selection or publication; curatorial approval is required before any artwork appears publicly.

No payment is requested for pilot participation. Details of application handling and privacy will be made available before registration opens.

For enquiries, please visit the contact page.
