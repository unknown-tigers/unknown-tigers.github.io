UNKNOWN / Artist submissions

Submit your work.

UNKNOWN welcomes distinctive practices across disciplines. We consider the work on its own terms — beyond reputation or visibility.

Send a short introduction and a link to your materials. No account or password is required. Submissions are free.

01 / Prepare

What to send

Artist

Your artist name, a short biography, and any relevant website or social links (optional).

Artworks

1–3 artworks. For each: title, medium, year if relevant, and a short description.

Images

1–5 images per artwork, preferably in high resolution. Place them in clearly named folders.

Drive link

Share a Google Drive folder or an equivalent accessible download link. Give unowntigers@gmail.com Viewer access, and check that download access works.

02 / Send

One email is enough.

Email your details, artwork descriptions and folder link. We may ask for improved photographs, additional context or corrections during the review.

Submit via Email

unowntigers@gmail.com

03 / Review

Curated, not automatic.

UNKNOWN reviews submissions individually. We may accept, decline or request revisions. Sending materials grants permission to assess them privately, not to publish them. The final selection, credits and publication permission are agreed with each artist before anything goes live.

Artists retain their rights. Please do not include passwords, identity documents, or other sensitive information.
