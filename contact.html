Terms & principles.

Draft for design and discussion — not yet adopted legal terms. Legal and privacy review are required before collecting real registrations or personal files.

1. Purpose and eligibility

UNKNOWN is a curatorially selected space for artistic work. The platform may review and decline submissions according to its published principles. Submission is not an entitlement to publication.

2. Intellectual property

Contributors retain ownership of their work. A separate, clearly worded permission will be requested for the specific images, audio, video and descriptive materials displayed on UNKNOWN. No broader transfer of rights is implied.

3. Review and accuracy

Artists are responsible for submitting materials they have the right to share and for supplying accurate credits. UNKNOWN can request revisions or withhold publication.

4. Updates and removal

Artists may request correction or removal of their profiles and works. The final workflow and applicable timelines will be published before live registration begins.

5. Exhibitions and commercial activity

Digital presentation does not authorize sales, commissions, shipping, physical exhibition, reproduction, exclusive representation or commercial licensing. Such activities require individual agreements.

6. Privacy and security

Real account registration will launch only after authentication, restricted file storage, data handling notices and appropriate retention and deletion procedures are implemented. No real data should be submitted to the current demonstration.
