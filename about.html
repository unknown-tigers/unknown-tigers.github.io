UNKNOWN / About

Art on its
own terms.

UNKNOWN is an independent international art platform dedicated to discovering and presenting distinctive creative voices.

We believe artistic value cannot be measured by reputation, professional status, commercial success, or visibility. Our curatorial approach values individuality, thoughtful experimentation, and the integrity of the work itself.

Across painting, sculpture, photography, sound, installation, moving image and other practices, UNKNOWN creates a considered space for artists to be encountered without being reduced to a single style or category.
