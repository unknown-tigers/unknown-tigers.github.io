Terms & principles.

UNKNOWN accepts submissions by email with a link to the artist’s own Drive folder or equivalent file-sharing service. No account is required, and submissions are free.

1. Materials

Send your artist name and a short biography; 1–3 works, each with a title, medium, year if relevant, and a brief description; and 1–5 images per work. Add optional website or social links.

2. File access

Share a folder with unowntigers@gmail.com using Viewer permission. Keep the original files in your own account and make sure UNKNOWN can access and download the materials.

3. Curatorial review

Submissions are considered individually. UNKNOWN may accept, decline, or request revisions, additional information or higher-resolution images. Submission alone does not guarantee publication.

4. Ownership and permission

Artists retain ownership. Submission grants permission for private curatorial review only. Publication requires separate confirmation of the final selection, credits and display permission. Commercial use and licensing require separate agreements.

5. Privacy and corrections

Do not share sensitive personal documents. Artists can contact UNKNOWN to ask about a submission, correct material, or request deletion of copies held by UNKNOWN.

6. Contact

Submit your work · unowntigers@gmail.com
